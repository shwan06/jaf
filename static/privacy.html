Privacy Policy

Русский от А до Я (Learn Russian from A to Z) · Last updated 29 August 2026

The short version. This app has no accounts, no analytics, no advertising, and no server of its own. Your study progress never leaves your device. The only time anything is sent anywhere is if you choose to use the optional AI Tutor or the microphone — both explained below.

What the app stores on your device

All of your learning data is kept locally in your browser or app storage (localStorage) and is never transmitted to the developer:

  • Which lessons you have completed and your flashcard review schedule.
  • Quiz, exam, listening and case-drill scores, streaks and XP.
  • Saved favourites, and your theme and translation-language preferences.
  • An API key, if you choose to enter one for the AI Tutor.

You can erase all of it at any time by clearing the app's storage, or by uninstalling the app. The "Export my progress" feature saves this data to a file you control; it deliberately excludes any API key.

The AI Tutor (optional)

The AI Tutor is switched off until you supply your own API key from a provider you choose (such as Google Gemini, OpenRouter, Groq, Mistral, Cerebras, NVIDIA, DeepSeek, Qwen, YandexGPT or Anthropic).

  • When you send a message, that message and the conversation so far are sent directly from your device to the provider you selected, using your key. It does not pass through any server belonging to this app.
  • Your API key is stored only on your device and is sent only to that same provider, as the authentication header their API requires.
  • Your messages are then handled under that provider's privacy policy and terms, not this one. Please read theirs before use.
  • The developer of this app never receives, sees, or stores your messages or your key.

Microphone

The Pronunciation trainer and the AI Tutor's voice-input button use the microphone, and only while you are actively using those features. On Android the app uses the operating system's own speech-recognition service to turn speech into text; depending on your device and settings that service may process audio on-device or on your device vendor's servers, under their privacy policy. This app does not record, save, or transmit audio itself, and no audio is ever sent to the developer.

The microphone permission is optional. The rest of the app works normally if you decline it.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or registration.
  • No analytics, tracking, advertising, or third-party SDKs of any kind.
  • No collection of your name, email, contacts, location, or device identifiers.
  • No selling or sharing of any data with anyone.

Children

The app is a general-audience language course and is not directed at children under 13. It collects no personal information from anyone, including children.

Links to other sites

Some listening lessons link to videos hosted on YouTube so you can hear native speakers. Those links open in your own browser or the YouTube app, and your use of them is covered by their privacy policies.

Changes

If this policy changes, the revised version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date.

Contact
